# Hermosa Medical Center – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Hermosa Medical Center is a multidisciplinary healthcare provider located in Muwaileh, Sharjah, UAE. The center offers dermatology, laser treatments, beauty services, dentistry, physiotherapy and additional wellness services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting Hermosa as a comprehensive medical center, helping patients discover the right department and making appointment enquiries more accessible. The design direction prioritises clear service organisation, patient-focused messaging and professional healthcare presentation.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key issues identified on the original website:

### 1. Unclear Homepage Messaging and Brand Positioning
- Replaced generic introductory messaging with a clearer, patient-focused headline and supporting value proposition.
- Communicated Hermosa's multidisciplinary healthcare offering more effectively.
- Strengthened the homepage introduction with a prominent appointment call to action.

### 2. Fragmented Service Presentation
- Organised dermatology, laser treatments, beauty, dentistry and physiotherapy into clearly defined service categories.
- Improved department presentation with dedicated sections, concise descriptions and direct links to relevant information.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify services according to their healthcare and wellness needs.

### 3. Practitioner Credibility and Booking Journey
- Introduced a more structured presentation of practitioner information, using verified professional details.
- Improved the visibility of practitioner profiles and relevant credentials.
- Integrated clearer booking and contact calls to action throughout the homepage to reduce unnecessary navigation.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Established a more polished and professional healthcare-focused appearance aligned with Hermosa's multidisciplinary positioning.
- **Layout and Structure:** Improved section hierarchy and department organisation to make the homepage easier to scan and navigate.
- **User Experience:** Created a clearer journey from the introductory message to service discovery, practitioner information and appointment enquiries.
- **Content Consistency:** Standardised the presentation of medical departments, service descriptions and practitioner information.
- **Overall Appearance:** Developed a more cohesive homepage that communicates Hermosa's healthcare offering through clearly organised content and consistent visual presentation.

## Project Summary

The redesigned Hermosa Medical Center homepage addresses three primary challenges: unclear brand messaging, fragmented service presentation and a disconnected practitioner credibility and booking journey. The result is a more structured, patient-focused website experience that communicates the center's multidisciplinary services and provides clearer pathways towards appointment enquiries.
